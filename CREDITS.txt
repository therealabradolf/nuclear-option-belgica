Belgica - a real-world map for Nuclear Option, made with Open Skies 0.1.0

Map id belgica, registered in the game as cm.belgica.10f62142
Centre 50.59348, 4.28467; 261.12 km square.
Airbases: Brussels (EBBR, Boscali), Ostend-Bruges (EBOS, Boscali), Brussels South (EBCI, Primeva), Maastricht Aachen (EHBK, Boscali), Liege (EBLG, Primeva), Kleine Brogel (EBBL, Boscali), Beauvechain (EBBE, Primeva), Gilze Rijen (EHGR, Boscali), Chievres (EBCV, Primeva), Woensdrecht (EHWO, Boscali), Kortrijk-Wevelgem (EBKT, Boscali), Merville-Calonne (LFQT, Primeva), Valenciennes-Denain (LFAV, Primeva), Antwerp (Deurne) (EBAW, Boscali), Charleville-Mezieres (LFQV, Primeva), Peronne (LFAG, Primeva)

Needs BepInEx 5 and NOCustomMaps 1.4 or later. Install by copying
  belgica.nomap  to  <Nuclear Option>\BepInEx\plugins\NOCustomMaps\maps\
  missions\Belgica - Escalation\  to  %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Shockfront\NuclearOption\Missions\
  missions\Belgica - Strike on Brussels\  to  %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Shockfront\NuclearOption\Missions\
  missions\Belgica - Strike on Liege\  to  %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Shockfront\NuclearOption\Missions\
  missions\Belgica Free Flight\  to  %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Shockfront\NuclearOption\Missions\
(Open Skies' Install button does both.) Every player in a multiplayer game needs this exact build.
